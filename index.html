Research Interests

My research interests in technical communication include:

  • User experience (UX) design
  • Instructional design
  • Science communication
  • The rhetoric of health and medicine
I am particularly interested in how communication practices and design strategies shape user understanding, accessibility, and engagement with complex information. Through these areas, I hope to explore how technical communication can improve learning experiences and make scientific and health-related information more accessible to diverse audiences.
